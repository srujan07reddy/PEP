Critical Architectural Rule:
Do not implement every possible engine before building PEP intelligence.
Tree-sitter and NLP++ are enough to prove the architecture:
- 2 engines
- 2 adapters
- 2 normalized models
- 1 evidence model
- 1 knowledge compiler
- PEP intelligence

Once that vertical slice works, adding the 10th or 20th engine becomes an integration problem rather than an architectural redesign.
